2. **Copied mtimes are preserved but unbound (#148 seam).** Native Windows used identical
   bytes/modes with different source mtimes; identity/run ID stayed equal while copied
   `st_mtime_ns` produced opposing effects. Thread `PRRT_kwDOTPn_4s6VToeS`:
   https://github.com/Chris0Jeky/agent-harness/pull/140#discussion_r3687800860
3. **POSIX descendants can leave the root process group (#145 seam).** WSL reproduced both normal
   completion and timeout leaving a `setpgrp` child alive in the same session but outside the root
   PGID, executing from a deleted snapshot. The two diagnostic child PIDs were explicitly killed
   and confirmed absent. Thread `PRRT_kwDOTPn_4s6VToeQ`:
   https://github.com/Chris0Jeky/agent-harness/pull/140#discussion_r3687800853

There is no small portable, dependency-free way in Python's standard library to signal an entire
POSIX session or enumerate it race-safely. `/proc`, cgroups, and subreapers are Linux-only;
external `ps` has portability and PID-race problems; Darwin needs a separate API. The smallest
honest v0 response is contract narrowing to root-process-group containment, with process-group or
session escape explicit. Do not claim complete process-family containment without a separate
cross-platform design.
